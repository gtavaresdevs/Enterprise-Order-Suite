# ADR-0020: Storefront PIX prepayment with manual confirmation, no PSP
- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-01 (proposed); 2026-10-06 (accepted)
- Decided by: Gabriel, who accepted it on 2026-10-06 (project thread, 2026-10-06T18:12Z: "4 - confirmed", "confirms all points as per recommended by claude"). Gabriel answered points 4 and 6 himself (quoted); the details he delegated again on 2026-10-06 ("apply best pratice") are Claude's decisions, marked as such.
- Supersedes: none. As target behaviour it replaces the storefront's self-confirming PIX mock (business rule 39; FE `PixWaitingMock.tsx`), which ADR-0005 already forbids wiring as a payment confirmation.

## Context
- Register Q-65 asked how online customers pay this run: a) on delivery or pickup (card terminal, cash, or PIX at the door; the order starts UNPAID); b) (a) plus a static PIX key shown at checkout, confirmed by staff by hand; c) other.
- Gabriel's answer (project thread, 2026-10-01T16:25Z): "Q65 - a -> but payment through pix should be in advanced, if storefront selects pix, we should have a pix payment route that displays the code, confirmation on both sides (customer and restaurant), and confirms order is placed and paid, payment with card etc in storefront comes later when the app is ready and functional as this involves integrating with payment providers. Not sure if this is an actual good apporach so I will leave you to decide what makes sense now for the app but can be done later once ready."
  - So: option a is the base, PIX is paid in advance at checkout, card payments in the storefront come later through a payment provider, and he delegated the approach to Claude.
- Constraints from Accepted decisions:
  - ADR-0005: payments are record-only. The app never processes, authorizes, captures, refunds or queues a payment, never generates dynamic PIX charges, and never marks a payment successful on its own (Decision 1, 4; Consequences). The self-confirming `PixWaitingMock` must not be wired as a confirmation.
  - ADR-0006: no PSP this run (Decision 2). Bringing one back needs a new ADR decided by Gabriel (Decision 4).
  - ADR-0003 C5: the app never queues payments.
  - ADR-0014 and Q-67 a (2026-10-01): restaurant settings are a typed backend schema; "accepted payment methods" is one of the ordering settings.
- Payment answers of 2026-10-01 ("Q33  - Q64- Recommended"):
  - Q-55 a: an order becomes Paid when staff record the payment (method, amount); manual entry is the only way this run; no provider API or webhook.
  - Q-58 a: a Payment record {id, orderId, method PIX/CARD/CASH, amount, recordedBy, recordedAt, optional provider and authorization code/NSU}, several per order; order `paymentStatus` = UNPAID, PAID or REFUNDED.
  - Q-56 a: a paid order can be cancelled; staff record the refund made outside the app and `paymentStatus` becomes REFUNDED.
  - Q-61 a: the KDS and order tracking keep polling.
- Gabriel's Q-70 answer (same message) ties pickup to PIX prepayment: "Q70 - pickup should only exist if within zone if paid by pix, or when in app payment is ready, if outside zone then only when the in app payment is ready, this avoids." It ends mid-sentence; Q-86 asked him to confirm the reading, and he did on 2026-10-06 (Q-86 a).
- Frontend today (mock only): a PIX/Card/Cash picker (FE `src/components/payment/PaymentMethodPicker.tsx` L15). `PixWaitingMock` shows a hardcoded mock BR Code and confirms itself after 2.8 s (FE `src/features/storefront/components/PixWaitingMock.tsx` L6-7, L13-16), rendered from `StorefrontFeature.tsx` L155 (business rule 39).
- A static PIX BR Code (the EMV "copia e cola" payload and its QR code) can carry the receiver's PIX key, merchant name and city, a fixed amount and a reference (txid), closed by a CRC16 checksum. Any Brazilian bank app pays it. No PSP or bank API is involved, and the money moves between the banks without passing through the app.
- Reading of the constraints: a static code is not a dynamic PIX charge in ADR-0005's sense (a dynamic code points to a charge created at a PSP or bank, which this ADR never creates), and it is not the PSP or gateway that ADR-0006 puts Out (the customer pays from their own bank app). Gabriel confirmed this reading when he accepted the ADR (2026-10-06).

## Decision
Accepted (Gabriel, 2026-10-06T18:12Z). Proposed by Claude on 2026-10-01 under his Q-65 delegation.

1. **Storefront payment choices this run:**
   - pay on delivery or pickup: card terminal or cash (Q-65 a), and PIX at the door (option a lists it; keeping it next to prepayment was Claude's proposal, confirmed by Gabriel with this ADR on 2026-10-06); the order starts UNPAID;
   - PIX prepayment at checkout.
   A restaurant offers only the choices in its "accepted payment methods" setting (Q-67 a).
2. **Static PIX code generated by the backend.** PIX prepayment uses a static PIX BR Code (EMV "copia e cola" payload plus QR code) that the backend generates from the restaurant's PIX key, a new typed restaurant setting (ADR-0014, Q-67). The code carries the exact order total as the amount and the order number as the reference (txid); merchant name and city come from restaurant data (point 7). The amount is the server-derived order total (D-16). No PSP, no bank API.
3. **Flow:**
   1. The customer places the order choosing PIX. The order is created (NEW) with `paymentStatus` UNPAID and flagged "PIX awaiting confirmation".
   2. The customer sees a payment page with the QR code, the copy-paste code, the amount and the order number. They may tap "Já paguei" ("I have paid"). That tap is customer-reported and never treated as proof of payment.
   3. Staff see the order flagged "PIX to confirm", check their own bank app, and record the payment (method PIX, amount; Q-55 a manual record, Q-58 a record shape). Recording it sets `paymentStatus` PAID.
   4. The customer's tracking page then shows "payment confirmed" (polling, Q-61 a).
   Both sides confirm, but only the restaurant's confirmation counts (ADR-0005 Decision 4).
4. **Unconfirmed PIX and the kitchen** (Gabriel, 2026-10-06T18:12Z):
   - Auto-cancel: an unconfirmed PIX order is cancelled automatically after 5 minutes (Gabriel: "5 minutes"). The cancel is a status change to CANCELLED like any other cancel; if the customer did pay, staff record the refund made outside the app and `paymentStatus` becomes REFUNDED (Q-56 a). What starts the clock and what counts as confirmed: point 8.
   - Kitchen: Gabriel: "NEVER START ORDERS IN KITCHEN WITHOUT PAYMENT CONFIRMATION OR OWNER CONFIRMATION, IF ITS PIX IN APP, IF ITS PAY AT HOME THEN OWNER WOULD HAVE ACCEPTED THE ORDER MAKING IT GO TO THE KITCHEN REGARDLESS". So a PIX-prepaid order reaches the kitchen only after staff record its payment or the owner confirms it; a pay-on-delivery or pay-on-pickup order reaches the kitchen once the owner accepts it, whatever its payment status. This replaces the 2026-10-01 default proposal (visible to the kitchen at once with an "unpaid PIX" badge). Staff still see an unconfirmed PIX order at once, flagged "PIX to confirm", so they can check their bank. How this maps onto the order statuses of Q-47 a: point 8.
   - Staff can cancel an unconfirmed PIX order before the 5 minutes run out, under the same Q-56 rules.
5. **Later, not this run:** automatic PIX confirmation and card payments in the storefront, through a payment-provider integration (integrate, never build; ADR-0005, ADR-0006), once the app is functional. Bringing in a provider needs a new ADR decided by Gabriel (ADR-0006 Decision 4).
6. **Pickup eligibility** (Q-86 a, Gabriel, 2026-10-06): this run, pickup is offered only when the customer prepays by PIX and is inside a delivery zone; outside every zone there is no pickup until in-app payment exists.
7. **PIX key, key types, merchant name and city.** Delegated to Claude on 2026-10-06 (Gabriel: "ACCEPT THE USUAL AND STORE WHERE BEST PRATICE"; "follow best practice"). Claude's decision is being researched and will be recorded here.
8. **How the order stores the payment option and the "Já paguei" report, and how the kitchen rule and the auto-cancel map onto the order.** Delegated to Claude on 2026-10-06 (Gabriel: "IDK apply best pratice from known practices."). Claude's decision is being researched and will be recorded here.

Rejected alternatives:
- Dynamic PIX through a PSP API, with automatic confirmation: out this run (ADR-0006); it is what point 5 brings later.
- Showing the static PIX key without amount or reference (Q-65 option b as worded): more matching errors for staff.
- Confirming the payment when the customer taps "Já paguei" or after a timer (today's mock): it reports an unconfirmed external operation as successful (ADR-0005 Decision 4).

## Consequences
- No PSP contract, fees, secrets, SDKs or webhooks now.
- The restaurant matches PIX transfers by hand. The exact amount and the order-number reference reduce the effort; a bank app may not show the reference to the receiver, so the amount and time matter too.
- Generating the EMV payload with its CRC16 checksum is a small backend utility, unit-tested against known-good payloads.
- The PIX key becomes restaurant data, scoped to its restaurant (ADR-0001), validated on the server and never kept in browser storage (ADR-0014).
- Must: offer PIX prepayment only when the restaurant accepts PIX and has a PIX key set.
- Must: build the code on the server from the stored order total; the client never supplies the amount.
- Must: keep the "Já paguei" tap free of payment state; it only tells staff what the customer reports.
- Never: set `paymentStatus` PAID without a staff-recorded payment; confirm on a timer or on the customer's tap; call a PSP or bank API; generate dynamic PIX charges (ADR-0005).
- Must: keep a PIX-prepaid order out of the kitchen until its payment is recorded or the owner confirms it (point 4).
- Risk of the 5-minute window (Claude's note, not a change to Gabriel's answer): a customer who pays near the end of the window can find the order cancelled before staff check the bank; staff then record the refund made outside the app (Q-56 a). Staff who confirm within the window avoid it.
- It is Build 4 work (ADR-0007); the Storefront contract cites it as Accepted.
