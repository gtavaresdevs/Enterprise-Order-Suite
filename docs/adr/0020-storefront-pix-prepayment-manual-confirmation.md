## Context
- Register Q-65 asked how online customers pay this run: a) on delivery or pickup (card terminal, cash, or PIX at the door; the order starts UNPAID); b) (a) plus a static PIX key shown at checkout, confirmed by staff by hand; c) other.
- Gabriel's answer (project thread, 2026-10-01T16:25Z): "Q65 - a -> but payment through pix should be in advanced, if storefront selects pix, we should have a pix payment route that displays the code, confirmation on both sides (customer and restaurant), and confirms order is placed and paid, payment with card etc in storefront comes later when the app is ready and functional as this involves integrating with payment providers. Not sure if this is an actual good apporach so I will leave you to decide what makes sense now for the app but can be done later once ready."
  - So: option a is the base, PIX is paid in advance at checkout, card payments in the storefront come later through a payment provider, and he delegated the approach to Claude.
- Constraints from Accepted decisions:
  - ADR-0005: payments are record-only. The app never processes, authorizes, captures, refunds or queues a payment, never generates dynamic PIX charges, and never marks a payment successful on its own (Decision 1, 4; Consequences). The self-confirming `PixWaitingMock` must not be wired as a confirmation.
  - ADR-0006: no PSP this run (Decision 2). Bringing one back needs a new ADR decided by Gabriel (Decision 4).
  - ADR-0003 C5: the app never queues payments.
  - ADR-0014 and Q-67 a (2026-10-01): restaurant settings are a typed backend schema; "accepted payment methods" is one of the ordering settings.
- Payment answers of 2026-10-01 ("Q33  - Q64- Recommended"):
  - Q-55 a: an order becomes Paid when staff record the payment (method, amount); manual entry is the only way this run; no provider API or webhook.
  - Q-58 a: a Payment record {id, orderId, method PIX/CARD/CASH, amount, recordedBy, recordedAt, optional provider and authorization code/NSU}, several per order; order `paymentStatus` = UNPAID, PAID or REFUNDED.
  - Q-56 a: a paid order can be cancelled; staff record the refund made outside the app and `paymentStatus` becomes REFUNDED.
  - Q-61 a: the KDS and order tracking keep polling.
- Gabriel's Q-70 answer (same message) ties pickup to PIX prepayment: "Q70 - pickup should only exist if within zone if paid by pix, or when in app payment is ready, if outside zone then only when the in app payment is ready, this avoids." It ends mid-sentence; Q-86 asked him to confirm the reading, and he did on 2026-10-06 (Q-86 a).
- Frontend today (mock only): a PIX/Card/Cash picker (FE `src/components/payment/PaymentMethodPicker.tsx` L15). `PixWaitingMock` shows a hardcoded mock BR Code and confirms itself after 2.8 s (FE `src/features/storefront/components/PixWaitingMock.tsx` L6-7, L13-16), rendered from `StorefrontFeature.tsx` L155 (business rule 39).
- A static PIX BR Code (the EMV "copia e cola" payload and its QR code) can carry the receiver's PIX key, merchant name and city, a fixed amount and a reference (txid), closed by a CRC16 checksum. Any Brazilian bank app pays it. No PSP or bank API is involved, and the money moves between the banks without passing through the app.
- Reading of the constraints: a static code is not a dynamic PIX charge in ADR-0005's sense (a dynamic code points to a charge created at a PSP or bank, which this ADR never creates), and it is not the PSP or gateway that ADR-0006 puts Out (the customer pays from their own bank app). Gabriel confirmed this reading when he accepted the ADR (2026-10-06).

## Decision
Accepted (Gabriel, 2026-10-06T18:12Z). Proposed by Claude on 2026-10-01 under his Q-65 delegation.

1. **Storefront payment choices this run:**
   - pay on delivery: card terminal or cash (Q-65 a), or PIX at the door (option a lists it; keeping it next to prepayment was Claude's proposal, confirmed by Gabriel with this ADR on 2026-10-06); the order starts UNPAID. Q-65 a also named pickup, but pickup is offered only with PIX prepayment (point 6, Q-86 a), so there is no pay-on-pickup storefront order this run;
   - PIX prepayment at checkout.
   A restaurant offers only the choices in its "accepted payment methods" setting (Q-67 a).
2. **Static PIX code generated by the backend.** PIX prepayment uses a static PIX BR Code (EMV "copia e cola" payload plus QR code) that the backend generates from the restaurant's PIX key, a new typed restaurant setting (ADR-0014, Q-67). The code carries the exact order total as the amount and the order number as the reference (txid); merchant name and city come from restaurant data (point 7). The amount is the server-derived order total (D-16). No PSP, no bank API.
3. **Flow:**
   1. The customer places the order choosing PIX. The order is created (NEW) with `paymentStatus` UNPAID and flagged "PIX awaiting confirmation".
   2. The customer sees a payment page with the QR code, the copy-paste code, the amount and the order number. They may tap "Já paguei" ("I have paid"). That tap is customer-reported and never treated as proof of payment; a tap before the 5 minutes run out keeps the order from being auto-cancelled (point 4, Q-88 a).
   3. Staff see the order flagged "PIX to confirm", check their own bank app, and record the payment (method PIX, amount; Q-55 a manual record, Q-58 a record shape). Recording it sets `paymentStatus` PAID.
   4. The customer's tracking page then shows "payment confirmed" (polling, Q-61 a).
   Both sides confirm, but only the restaurant's confirmation counts (ADR-0005 Decision 4).
4. **Unconfirmed PIX and the kitchen** (Gabriel, 2026-10-06T18:12Z):
   - Auto-cancel: an unconfirmed PIX order is cancelled automatically after 5 minutes (Gabriel: "5 minutes"). The cancel is a status change to CANCELLED like any other cancel; if the customer did pay, staff record the refund made outside the app and `paymentStatus` becomes REFUNDED (Q-56 a). What starts the clock and what counts as confirmed: point 8.
     - Decided later (Q-88 a, 2026-10-07): if the customer taps "Já paguei" before the 5 minutes run out, the order is not auto-cancelled. It waits, still out of the kitchen, until staff confirm the payment, release the order or cancel it. Gabriel chose "Hold after tap" on a decision card (decision cards, project thread, 2026-10-07T00:01Z): "After a tap the order waits for staff to confirm or cancel it; a false tap only leaves an order that staff cancel."
   - Kitchen: Gabriel: "NEVER START ORDERS IN KITCHEN WITHOUT PAYMENT CONFIRMATION OR OWNER CONFIRMATION, IF ITS PIX IN APP, IF ITS PAY AT HOME THEN OWNER WOULD HAVE ACCEPTED THE ORDER MAKING IT GO TO THE KITCHEN REGARDLESS". So a PIX-prepaid order reaches the kitchen only after staff record its payment or the owner confirms it; a pay-on-delivery ("PAY AT HOME") order reaches the kitchen once the owner accepts it, whatever its payment status. This replaces the 2026-10-01 default proposal (visible to the kitchen at once with an "unpaid PIX" badge). Staff still see an unconfirmed PIX order at once, flagged "PIX to confirm", so they can check their bank. How this maps onto the order statuses of Q-47 a: point 8.
     - Decided later (Q-87 a, 2026-10-07): any role that handles orders (owner, manager, staff; Q-17 a) accepts a pay-at-home order, and only the owner and the manager release an unpaid PIX order to the kitchen. Gabriel chose "Staff accept" on a decision card (decision cards, project thread, 2026-10-07T00:01Z): "Owner, manager and staff accept pay-at-home orders; only the owner and the manager release an unpaid PIX order." The card also stated that, under every option, an order staff create themselves (phone, table) counts as accepted at creation.
   - Staff can cancel an unconfirmed PIX order before the 5 minutes run out, or while a tap holds it, under the same Q-56 rules.
5. **Later, not this run:** automatic PIX confirmation and card payments in the storefront, through a payment-provider integration (integrate, never build; ADR-0005, ADR-0006), once the app is functional. Bringing in a provider needs a new ADR decided by Gabriel (ADR-0006 Decision 4).
6. **Pickup eligibility** (Q-86 a, Gabriel, 2026-10-06): this run, pickup is offered only when the customer prepays by PIX and is inside a delivery zone; outside every zone there is no pickup until in-app payment exists.
7. **PIX key, merchant name and city.** Claude's decision (2026-10-06) under Gabriel's delegation of the same day: "ACCEPT THE USUAL AND STORE WHERE BEST PRATICE" for the key, "follow best practice" for the name and city. Gabriel may override it.
   - Key types: all five that the DICT (the Central Bank's PIX key directory) accepts: CPF, CNPJ, phone, email and random key (EVP). The owner picks the type and the server never guesses it, because a mobile number typed without +55 has the 11-digit shape of a CPF. The screen recommends a CNPJ or random key: a CPF, phone or email key travels in plain text inside every code customers receive. With any key type the customer's bank still shows the account holder's name, so no key type makes the owner anonymous.
   - Validation runs on the server and checks the format only. The app cannot check that a key exists or belongs to the restaurant (no DICT, PSP or bank access; ADR-0005, ADR-0006). The key is stored in the exact form the DICT uses and the code carries (at most 77 characters):
     - CPF: 11 digits, valid check digits, not all equal;
     - CNPJ: 14 characters, 12 letters or digits (stored uppercase) and 2 check digits, computed as the Receita Federal specifies for the alphanumeric CNPJ issued since July 2026; the same routine validates numeric CNPJs;
     - phone: a Brazilian mobile number in E.164 form (`+55`, area code, `9`, 8 digits); spaces, brackets, a leading `0` or `55` are normalized away;
     - email: lowercased, at most 77 characters, matching the DICT's own pattern, copied verbatim from the DICT API spec when the Storefront contract is written;
     - random key: a lowercase UUID with hyphens.
     A failure returns 400 with a field error and a SCREAMING_SNAKE code (D14); the Storefront contract writes the messages.
   - Storage: a typed, restaurant-scoped setting (ADR-0001, ADR-0014), built in Build 4 on the settings schema that Build 1 creates (Q-30 a): key type, normalized key, merchant name, merchant city, and who changed it and when (`Instant`). Columns of the settings schema or a 1:1 table: the Tenancy & Identity and Storefront contracts choose. Only the owner writes the key, through its own endpoint (`@PreAuthorize`), and no other settings payload carries it. The owner reads it in their restaurant (membership, Q-26 a); the platform admin reads it for support (Q-18 b, settings proposal A0). The public storefront shows only whether PIX prepayment is available (PIX accepted and a key set); the key reaches a customer only inside the code of their own order, with a leak test (LR-4). Never in browser storage; CPF, phone and email keys are masked in logs.
   - Change control: only the owner sets, changes or removes the key (settings proposal A5). Every change writes a restaurant audit event (B5) with who, when, and the old and new type and value; managers, who also see the audit log, see the values masked. After a change the screen shows a test code with no amount and asks the owner to scan it in a bank app (from another phone if their own bank refuses a self-payment) and check the recipient name; the check is advice, not a gate. Removing the key hides PIX prepayment at once and, because pickup needs PIX prepayment (Q-86 a), pickup too; the screen says so. Orders already placed keep their code.
   - Merchant name: the code's recipient-name field, at most 25 characters, labelled "Nome do recebedor (como aparece no seu banco)" and prefilled from the store name (Q-67 a) for the owner to correct. The customer's bank ignores this field and shows the name registered for the key, so a wrong value cannot send money elsewhere; the payment page shows it with "confira o nome no seu banco".
   - Merchant city: entered by the owner, required once a key is set, at most 15 characters. No other setting holds a city (Q-27 a, Q-67 a).
   - Building the code (point 2): a static code in the BCB format, with these fields in order: payload format `01`; no point-of-initiation field; merchant account `br.gov.bcb.pix` plus the key and nothing else; category `0000`; currency `986`; amount; country `BR`; name; city; reference; checksum.
     - The amount is the server-derived order total with two decimals and a dot, never formatted with a locale.
     - The reference (txid) is the order number (Q-50 a), digits only; staff see the same number on the order.
     - Name and city are converted when the code is built: accents removed, uppercased, only A-Z, 0-9 and spaces kept, spaces collapsed, cut to 25 or 15 characters, trimmed. A value that converts to nothing is rejected when saved, and the screen previews the converted form.
     - The checksum is CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE over the payload up to and including `6304`, written as four uppercase hex digits, unit-tested against the BCB's published example payload.
8. **The order's payment fields, the "Já paguei" report, acceptance and the auto-cancel.** Claude's decision (2026-10-06); Gabriel may override any of it.
   - The payment option, the chosen method and the "Já paguei" report: under Gabriel's delegation of the same day ("IDK apply best pratice from known practices.").
   - Acceptance, the auto-cancel mechanism and the line lock: Claude's design to carry out Gabriel's point 4 rules, which point 4 refers here; that delegation did not cover them. The refinements of Q-58 a and Q-59 a are Claude's, on rows Claude owns.
   - Gabriel's own answers of 2026-10-07 (decision cards, project thread, 2026-10-07T00:01Z): who accepts and who releases (Q-87 a), whether a "Já paguei" tap holds the order (Q-88 a), and records on CANCELLED and COMPLETED orders (Q-89 a). They are recorded below as "Decided later".
   - Field, event and error names are proposals; the Order Core and Storefront contracts fix them.
   - The customer's choice, set at creation and never changed (Q-48 a, rule 46):
     - the payment option: PIX prepayment, or pay on handover (at the door or the counter; delivery versus pickup is already the channel, Q-49 a). Staff-created orders are pay on handover;
     - the chosen method: PIX, card or cash; credit or debit for a card (rule 17); an optional "troco para" amount for cash, not below the total, from which the server works out the change (rule 41, D-16).
     The Order Core service checks at creation that the method is one the restaurant accepts (Q-67 a), that PIX prepayment has PIX accepted and a key set, and that a storefront pickup order is PIX prepayment inside a delivery zone (Q-86 a).
   - A PIX-prepayment order also keeps a copy of the code issued at creation, so a later key change never alters it, and a payment deadline: its server creation time plus 5 minutes. A replayed or late offline submission (Q-60 a) never moves it.
   - Payments stay the records of Q-58 a, with one refinement: each record is a payment or a refund (Q-56 a). The method recorded can differ from the chosen one (a customer who chose cash pays by card at the door); Claude's reading of Q-48 a is that rule 46 fixes the method the customer chose, not the method recorded at handover. `paymentStatus` is recomputed with each record: REFUNDED when a refund exists and payments minus refunds is zero or less; otherwise PAID when the payments reach the total; otherwise UNPAID. So a partial refund leaves a paid order PAID.
   - CANCELLED and COMPLETED orders are read-only (Q-48 a). A refund record may be added to a cancelled order (Q-56 a).
     - Decided later (Q-89 a, 2026-10-07): payment and refund records may still be added to CANCELLED and COMPLETED orders; the order and its lines stay read-only. Gabriel chose "Allow records" on a decision card (decision cards, project thread, 2026-10-07T00:01Z): "Staff can add payment and refund records to those orders, so the app's records match the bank." So a payment taken at handover can be recorded after the order is completed, and a refund after delivery.
   - "Já paguei": the order keeps the time of the customer's first tap; later taps change nothing. The tap publishes an event that writes the feed entry "customer says PIX paid" (settings proposal E4). The public endpoint finds the order by `/r/{slug}` and its ULID (Q-26 a, Q-69 a), is rate-limited, returns 404 for another restaurant's order (leak test, LR-4), and accepts the tap only for a storefront PIX-prepayment order that is NEW, UNPAID, not accepted and not past its deadline. The tap never creates a payment and never changes `paymentStatus` or acceptance; a tap before the deadline stops the auto-cancel (Q-88 a). A tap and the auto-cancel at the same moment go through the same guarded update, so one wins and the other gets a clear 409 (Claude's design). The customer page says "Aguardando confirmação do restaurante" until the order is PAID.
   - Acceptance carries the kitchen rule of point 4. It is recorded as facts on the order (when, by whom, how: created by staff, accepted by staff, payment recorded, released without payment), not as a new status, so Q-47 a and Q-48 a stand.
     - Staff-created orders are accepted at creation (the Q-87 card stated this under every option; Gabriel chose option a on 2026-10-07).
     - A storefront pay-on-handover order is accepted when the restaurant taps "Aceitar", whatever its payment status.
     - A PIX-prepayment order is accepted when a payment that makes it PAID is recorded while it is NEW, or when it is released without payment.
     - Order Core refuses NEW to PREPARING for an order that is not accepted (409, `ORDER_NOT_ACCEPTED`). The KDS and the Home kitchen backlog (rule 79) show only accepted orders, and the KDS new-order sound (settings proposal E3) plays when an accepted order first appears there.
     - Who may accept and who may release without payment, decided later (Q-87 a, 2026-10-07; point 4): any role that handles orders (owner, manager, staff) accepts a storefront pay-on-handover order; only the owner and the manager release an unpaid PIX order without payment. Any role that records payments (Q-55 a) makes a PIX order accepted by recording its payment. Every one of these actions is authorized in `@PreAuthorize` only (D2).
   - Staff see unconfirmed PIX orders in a "PIX a confirmar" lane of the Orders screen, never on the KDS: number, exact amount, customer name, time placed, a countdown to the auto-cancel; after a tap, "Cliente informou pagamento às hh:mm" replaces the countdown and the order waits for staff (Q-88 a). Actions: confirm payment (records a PIX payment with the total prefilled), release without payment (owner or manager, Q-87 a), cancel.
   - Auto-cancel (point 4): "unconfirmed" means not accepted. A scheduled job using the injected clock cancels every NEW, not-accepted PIX-prepayment order past its deadline that has no "Já paguei" tap (Q-88 a), through the Order Core service, restaurant by restaurant inside an explicit system context that the Tenancy & Identity contract defines (ADR-0001 fails closed). A guarded update makes a staff confirmation or a customer's tap at the same moment either win or get a clear 409. The cancel is recorded as made by the system with the reason "PIX not confirmed", so Analytics can count it apart (Q-62 a).
     - After a cancel or a payment the payment page hides the code. After a cancel it says the order was cancelled because the payment was not confirmed within 5 minutes and links the restaurant's WhatsApp (rule 56).
     - If money arrives for a cancelled order, the order stays CANCELLED (ADR-0003 C3, Q-48 a). Staff find it by number, record the PIX payment and then the refund made outside the app (Q-89 a), and it becomes REFUNDED (Q-56 a). It is never accepted.
     - The 5 minutes are Gabriel's value ("5 minutes"); keeping it fixed rather than an owner setting is Claude's reading of his single number. Pay-on-handover orders have no auto-cancel, and an order held by a tap has no second time limit; none was decided, so staff cancel a held order when no payment arrives.
   - Line edits: while a PIX-prepayment order is not accepted (at most 5 minutes, or longer while a tap holds it, Q-88 a), its lines are locked, so the issued code keeps matching the total. This narrows Q-59 a for that window only (Claude's refinement of a row Claude owns; Gabriel may override). Otherwise Q-59 a applies, and `paymentStatus` is recomputed after an edit.
   - Market basis: the Open Delivery standard (Abrasel, v1.7.1) separates what the customer chose (prepaid or paid on delivery, the method, "change for") from what was paid, and confirms an order before it is prepared; Brazilian ordering apps such as Cardápio Web make accepting an order a separate step.

Rejected alternatives:
- Dynamic PIX through a PSP API, with automatic confirmation: out this run (ADR-0006); it is what point 5 brings later.
- Showing the static PIX key without amount or reference (Q-65 option b as worded): more matching errors for staff.
- Confirming the payment when the customer taps "Já paguei" or after a timer (today's mock): it reports an unconfirmed external operation as successful (ADR-0005 Decision 4).

## Consequences
- No PSP contract, fees, secrets, SDKs or webhooks now.
- The restaurant matches PIX transfers by hand. The exact amount and the order-number reference reduce the effort; a bank app may not show the reference to the receiver, so the amount and time matter too.
- Generating the EMV payload with its CRC16 checksum is a small backend utility, unit-tested against known-good payloads (point 7).
- The PIX key becomes restaurant data, scoped to its restaurant (ADR-0001), validated on the server and never kept in browser storage (ADR-0014).
- Must: offer PIX prepayment only when the restaurant accepts PIX and has a PIX key set.
- Must: build the code on the server from the stored order total; the client never supplies the amount.
- Must: keep the "Já paguei" tap free of payment state; it only tells staff what the customer reports.
- Never: set `paymentStatus` PAID without a staff-recorded payment; confirm on a timer or on the customer's tap; call a PSP or bank API; generate dynamic PIX charges (ADR-0005).
- Must: keep a PIX-prepaid order out of the kitchen until its payment is recorded or the owner or a manager releases it (point 4, Q-87 a).
- Risk of the 5-minute window (Claude's note, not a change to Gabriel's answer): a customer who pays near the end of the window and does not tap "Já paguei" can find the order cancelled before staff check the bank; staff then record the payment and the refund made outside the app (Q-56 a, Q-89 a). A tap holds the order (Q-88 a), and staff who confirm within the window avoid it.
- A false "Já paguei" tap holds an order until staff cancel it. Held orders stay in the "PIX a confirmar" lane, out of the kitchen (Q-88 a).
- A static code never expires. A customer can pay it after the auto-cancel, or pay it twice; each such payment is refunded by hand (Q-56 a). Hiding the code after a cancel and showing the countdown reduce it but cannot prevent it.
- The app checks a key's format only. A typo that happens to match another real key sends customers' money there; the test scan, the recipient-name hint and the audit log are the safeguards (point 7).
- Not verified on 2026-10-06: whether every bank app already pays a static code whose key is an alphanumeric CNPJ (the DICT accepts the format since July 2026). The DICT email pattern is copied verbatim from the DICT API spec before it is coded.
- Point 8's order-side rules (acceptance and `ORDER_NOT_ACCEPTED`, payment and refund records, `paymentStatus` from the records, the KDS showing only accepted orders) are Order Core work, Build 3, under Q-87 a and Q-89 a. The rest is Build 4 work (ADR-0007). The Order Core and Storefront contracts cite it as Accepted.
